About Us

Our Mission: Providing culturally authentic South African meals to students and staff.


Ubuntu Bites is more than just a food stall; it's a "home away from home" experience. We understand that many students are living away from home for the first time. We aim to bridge that gap with the comfort of warm, traditional food and a welcoming atmosphere.
